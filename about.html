Making immersive skills accessible.

We're a Singapore-based school built on one belief: the best way to learn XR is to build with it, guided by people who've already shipped it.

Our story

BENIOS Education started when a small group of XR practitioners noticed the same gap again and again: plenty of hype about the metaverse, spatial computing and AR, but very few places where a beginner in Singapore could actually learn to build it, with real feedback and a clear path.

So we built the program we wished we'd had. Structured tracks, real projects, and mentors who care whether you finish. No fluff, no 40-hour video dumps you'll never watch, just a focused route from "I'm curious" to "I shipped this."

What we believe

  • Build first. You learn immersive tech by making immersive things, not by reading about them.
  • People over platforms. Mentorship and community beat any single tool or engine.
  • Access matters. Great XR education shouldn't be locked behind a university degree or a corporate budget.
  • Local, but global. We're proudly rooted in Singapore's growing tech scene, with skills that travel anywhere.
Our mission: equip 10,000 people across Southeast Asia with practical, portfolio-ready immersive-tech skills by 2030.

Who it's for

  • Students and career-switchers curious about AR/VR/XR
  • Designers and developers wanting to add spatial skills
  • Teams and companies upskilling for immersive projects
  • Educators and creatives exploring new mediums
