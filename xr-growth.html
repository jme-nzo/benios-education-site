Why the momentum?

  • Infrastructure is ready. Nationwide 5G and a strong digital backbone make immersive experiences practical at scale.
  • Talent is scarce. Demand for people who can design and build XR is outpacing supply, good news if you have the skills.
  • Support exists. Public initiatives and private investment continue to back immersive and deep-tech projects.
  • Hardware is maturing. Lighter, cheaper, more capable headsets and AR glasses are lowering the barrier to entry.
