A friendly note on VR headsets

Our VR headsets run on the Meta Quest 3 system, which reduces motion discomfort and offers safer engagement. Students can create VR Art in passthrough mode, where they create while still seeing their physical surroundings.

Frequently asked questions

Will my child experience motion sickness from using the VR?

Our VR headsets are equipped with the Meta Quest 3 system, which significantly reduces discomfort during use. The headsets are also lightweight, with improved ergonomics and accessibility.

Will my child experience claustrophobia during use?

We allow students to create VR Art in passthrough mode, which lets them see their physical surroundings while they create.

Can my child join even with no VR experience?

Yes — all are welcome, no prior VR experience needed.

What age range of students do you accept?

7 to 18 years old.
